Privacy policy

Last updated 18 November 2026

This page explains what information urelimitless collects when you message us or a business that uses our Sales Desk WhatsApp assistant, what we do with it, and how to have it deleted. Questions: work@urelimitless.com.

Who we are

urelimitless is an agency that serves clients worldwide. We build and run Sales Desk, an assistant that answers WhatsApp messages for businesses. When you message urelimitless, we decide how your information is used. When you message a business that uses Sales Desk, that business decides, and we process the information for them.

What we collect

  • Your WhatsApp phone number and profile name.
  • The messages, voice notes and pictures you send, and the replies sent to you.
  • Details you give in the chat, such as your business, what you want to buy, your delivery area or an order.
  • If you came from a Facebook or Instagram ad, which ad it was. WhatsApp tells us this when the chat starts.
  • For businesses that turn it on: bank alert emails, used only to match a payment to an order.

We do not collect your WhatsApp contacts, your other chats, or anything outside the conversation.

How we use it

  • To answer your questions, quote prices, take orders and confirm payments.
  • To remember what you told us, so you don't have to repeat it.
  • To pass your chat to a person when you ask for one, or when the assistant isn't sure.
  • To send up to two follow-up messages if a conversation stops. Reply STOP at any time and we stop.
  • To show the business which ads bring real customers.

We do not sell your information, and we do not use it for anyone else's advertising.

Who else handles it

  • Meta (WhatsApp Business Platform) carries the messages between you and us.
  • Meta's AI models (Meta Model API) read your message, voice note or picture to write the reply. Voice notes are turned into text for this.
  • Our hosting provider stores the conversation on servers we control.
  • The business you messaged, if it isn't urelimitless, sees the conversation and your order.

Because we serve clients in many countries, your information may be handled outside the country you live in, including where our team works and wherever Meta and our hosting provider run their servers.

How long we keep it

We keep a conversation for up to 12 months after your last message, then delete it, unless you are a customer and we need the order record. You can ask us to delete it sooner.

Deleting your information

To have your information deleted, email work@urelimitless.com with the phone number you used.

We delete your chats, notes and details within 30 days and confirm by email or WhatsApp. Messages already delivered to a business's own phone or records must be deleted by that business; we will pass your request to them.

Your rights

You can ask to see the information we hold about you, correct it, delete it, or stop follow-up messages. Email work@urelimitless.com and we will reply within 30 days. You can also complain to the data protection authority in your country.

Changes

If we change this policy, we update the date at the top of this page.
