# Surface: urelimitless.com home page

Scope: the whole marketing site as one page (hero, services, how it works, projects, contact). Mode: Persuade.

Audience: a company decision-maker (founder, ops or product lead) arriving from a referral or demo link, laptop or phone. Job: understand in seconds that urelimitless picks and wires AI tools and supplies the people who deliver, trust it from real shipped work, and start a conversation.

Action: "Patch us in", a short request form (company, what you need, tools already in use, email). No backend: mailto fallback plus an optional free form endpoint constant.

Proof: five real projects (Ringleader, Stash, Conviction, Cadence, My Bots) from PRODUCT.md. No clients, testimonials, numbers or pricing may be invented.

Constraints: free tiers only; reduced motion respected; AA contrast; name written as one word, lowercase.

## Direction contract

THESIS: urelimitless is the operator at a telephone exchange: companies on one side of the board, AI tools and skilled people on the other, and the agency is the hand that patches the cord through. The page refuses the category default (centred gradient headline, logo row, bento of same-size feature cards on navy) and the opposite default (white SaaS grid).

OWN-WORLD: warm bakelite near-black ground with a soft radial sheen, rendered panels one step lighter, brass jack rings with bevel, cream traffolyte label plates with engraved uppercase text, cloth-braided cords in signal red, exchange blue and lamp amber, small jewel lamps that light when a line is live. Display: Big Shoulders Display 900, cream. Labels: Barlow Condensed uppercase, tracked. Body: Barlow. Toll-ticket entries: Special Elite. Controls are physical: toggles flick with travel, buttons depress, lamps light. No gradient text, no glass, no neon edges.

STORY: the visitor sees a live board patching a company to a tool to a person; reads that the agency does both halves (tools and people); sees the four lines the exchange operates; sees how a call goes through; sees five completed calls that are real products; rings the operator.

FIRST VIEWPORT: header strip as a brass nameplate (urelimitless) with engraved label plates as nav and a lit "Patch us in" lamp button. Left 5/12: headline "Your company, patched through to the right tools and people." in display caps at about 6rem, one-paragraph sub, primary "Patch us in" and secondary "See completed calls". Right 7/12: the patch bay panel, three labelled jack columns (Companies, Tools, People); cords draw themselves from a company jack to a tool jack to a person jack, lamps light, then a new call patches through. Hovering or focusing a jack patches that line by hand.

FORM: candidate 1 of my ordered grounded list (the user took IMPECCABLE'S PICK over the roll's candidate 6, the Work Order Pad); seed key 839b9083. Code-led build; the pick sketch at .impeccable/decision/pick.html is the composition to match and the critique reference.

Signature interaction: the cord draw (SVG pathLength with a catenary sag) and jack lamps, orchestrated as one looping call sequence, interruptible by hover. Motion grammar elsewhere: toggles with travel on the services panel, a marquee of engraved tool labels, tickets settling in on scroll. Reduced motion: cords static and lit, nothing loops.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance.
